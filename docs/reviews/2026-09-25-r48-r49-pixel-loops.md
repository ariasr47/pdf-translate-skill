# R-48 and R-49: exact counts in C, and the oracle judges only its pages — 25 September 2026

**Assignment.** R-48 with R-49 is the second performance item, priority 6 of
the assignment. The request of record is the app's
`REQUEST-to-skill-review-2026-09-24`. The findings are R-48 and R-49 in
`docs/REVIEW-2026-09-24.md`.

The bar for performance items:
- a before-and-after measurement;
- output identical, shown by a byte or render diff;
- at the product's request (25 September), evidence on the app's own call
  shape:
  - `extract_segments` writing to an outdir, compared byte for byte;
  - `strip_text` with and without a `widget_text` mapping on FL-150;
  - one CJK target;
  - one `/Rotate` source.

It ships as v77, stacked on #47. It changes the internals of
`extract_segments`, `strip_text`, `verify` and `prepare_font`, not their
signatures. `invisible_text_pages` gains an optional `pages=`.

## What changed

**R-48.** Four Python loops over every byte of a render were copied into
four modules, with their thresholds.
- **Dark pixels.** The count is now `_pixels.dark_pixels`: the first
  channels are sliced out, and the dark ones are counted with
  `bytes.translate`. Verify's `ink`, extract's visible-content check, the
  invisible-text oracle and `prepare_font`'s render check all use it.
  `VISIBLE_INK` and `INK_SKIP` are defined once.
- **Changed pixels.** The oracle's before/after comparison
  (`_pixels.changed_channels`) compares 1,024-byte chunks and skips equal
  ones. It stops once the partial count proves the page cannot be flagged.
  The count only grows, and only a flagged page reports its fraction, so
  the output is unchanged.
- **Scripts.** `scripts_in` runs its loop only on the characters one
  compiled class matches.

**R-49.** `invisible_text_pages(src, pages=None)` judges only the given
pages, and extract passes the pages it was asked for. The strip it compares
against is still of the whole document. Verify's call stays whole-document
and independent, as the review advised unless Rodrigo rules otherwise.

**Not done, and why:**
- **Stripping only the wanted pages.** The review's verifier showed it is
  not exact: text left on any page refuses the whole file, and a form
  XObject two pages share is stripped once. So a slice of a document whose
  strip dominates stays slow: a 5-page slice of the IRS booklet still pays
  its 11.8 s strip.
- **Stripping in memory.** Measured on the stripped IRS booklet (3.8 MB):
  a save and reopen takes 366 ms through a temporary file and 169 ms through
  `BytesIO`. That is about 0.2 s a call. It would mean changing
  `strip_text`'s file contract (write, reopen, delete on leftover text),
  which is on the app's route, so it was left.
- **Reusing extract's span areas.** Extract's text dict is mapped to the
  unrotated space, so on a rotated page its areas could differ in the last
  float bit. With the page filter, the oracle only measures the wanted pages
  anyway.
- **Pillow.** The review measured Pillow as faster at comparing pixels. It
  is installed only because pikepdf needs it, and a library should not
  lean on a transitive dependency.

## Identical output

All of this was run on an M1 Pro. The harness
(`dev/probes/r48_r49_bench.py` and `r48_r49_compare.py`) ran on `git
archive` copies of `a05efbe` (before) and `8949a16` (after), each in its
own process. Raw data: `docs/reviews/data/2026-09-25-r48-r49/`.

- **Every output of 34 inputs, 311 files, is byte-identical.** Only each
  tree's path prefix and a PDF's trailer `/ID` are normalised. The inputs
  are the 17 wild PDFs, the 16 corpus PDFs and a constructed 40-page job.
  The outputs compared:
  - `extract_segments` writing to an outdir, for the whole document and
    pages 1–5;
  - the oracle's result;
  - `strip_text`, its report and its PDF, plain and with a filled
    `widget_text` mapping on every form with widgets;
  - `run_verify` of each original against itself;
  - `prepare_font`'s render check.
- **The scoped oracle** equals the whole-document verdict cut to pages 1–5,
  on all 9 documents of 10 pages or more.
- **FL-150** (the source recorded in its job README, sha256 matching): all
  15 files are identical, including the strip with a filled `widget_text`
  mapping. The job's own `widget_text.json` from 17 September is keyed by
  partial field names, which R-03 refuses, and both trees refuse it the
  same way.
- **A CJK target:** a Japanese delivery built through `prepare_font` and
  `retypeset` gets the same verdict from both trees, all 17 gates.
- **A `/Rotate` source:** `corpus/rotated.pdf` is among the 34 inputs.
- **The 17-PDF wild probe** (`dev/wild/probe.py`) gives the same results on
  both trees, once timings and paths are set aside.

## Independent verification

Two passes on a different model (Sonnet) set out to refute the change by
running code, on the same two archive copies.

- **Exactness: confirmed.** Each new count was compared with the old loops,
  copied from the base:
  - bytes, bytearray and memoryview inputs, and real pixmaps: grey, RGB and
    CMYK, with and without alpha, n = 1 to 5;
  - empty and odd-length buffers, unequal lengths, and chunk boundaries;
  - every threshold edge;
  - 200 early-stop trials near the 3% line, and cases built to land exactly
    on it;
  - every `SCRIPT_RANGES` boundary, 500 random strings over all planes,
    and lone surrogates.

  Every result matched.
- **Found, minor:** a one-shot generator passed as `pages=` was used up by
  the first membership test. The old filter in extract had the same trap,
  and extract passes a set, so no output changed. Fixed in `64c5d30`, with
  the test above.
- **Outputs: confirmed.** The second pass built 11 PDFs of its own:
  - an OCR layer, and visible text over an image;
  - a 20-page dark-pixel sweep across `INK_SKIP` and `VISIBLE_INK`;
  - a page straddling the 3% line;
  - a `/Rotate 90` page, and a form XObject two pages share;
  - CJK, Devanagari and widget pages, and a page with only vector ink;
  - a 9-page mix.

  It added five wild PDFs and FL-150. On all of them, 132 artifacts were
  identical across the two trees, with no exceptions: extract outputs, the
  oracle and 85 scoped checks, strip with and without `widget_text`,
  verify verdicts, the loud console output of `extract_segments.main` and
  `verify.main`, and `prepare_font`'s render check on Latin, CJK and Arabic
  faces.
