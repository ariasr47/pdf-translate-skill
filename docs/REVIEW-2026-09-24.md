## Summary

The shipped package is in fair shape. The 695-test suite passes apart from the known macOS-only cwd test, most refusals are loud and typed, and the repository was not changed at any point. Merging 179 verified findings from twelve review areas gives **110 items (5 high, 49 medium, 56 low)**, plus 19 notes that add evidence to rows already in the backlog.

The items that matter most are silent defects on the default legacy path:
- `strip_text` deletes colour and graphics state set inside text objects, so shading and rules change colour on 4 of 17 real PDFs.
- The 4.0 pt size clamp lets small print overlap its neighbour while the scale gate reports it fine.
- Widget text keyed by partial field name gives 72 widgets on five government forms another field's accessible name.

A second group is verdicts that are wrong in one direction or the other:
- The leak scan passes untranslated text in unrecognised or accented-Latin sources, and fails honest translations on the documented `--source-words-from` command.
- Gate 18 and the fill round-trip can PASS without judging what they claim to judge.
- A stale `scale_report.json` can turn a shrunken build into a PASS.

typography-1 is not yet usable on real documents. It refuses all 17 wild PDFs, and it only does so after the mapping is authored. Verifying a finished typography form also takes 2–4 GB, because of one misplaced line.

Speed is dominated by three things: pure-Python pixel loops, a whole-document scan oracle that ignores `--pages` and reruns in every rebuild, and full-face CJK font instancing, which is about 79% of Linux CI time.

Outside the code:
- The repository has been public since 2 September, while A20 still treats sanitation as a pre-publication chore.
- The cold-start documents are stale.
- The parity ratchets and the raw review evidence live in unguarded dev tooling and ignored folders, on a machine with no backup.

Sizes: XS is one focused edit, S a bounded change, M several files or a new measurement, L broad. Severity and evidence are the verifier's wherever it differed from the reviewer's.

## Do first

Ranked by impact against size. All were reproduced by a verifier.

1. **R-01: strip keeps the graphics state set inside text objects.**
   - **Where:** `pdf-translate/pdf_translate/strip_text.py:112-127`. The drop is at `:124-125`; the same function strips Form XObjects at `:187`.
   - **Why:** BT/ET do not save and restore state, so dropping `rg`/`g`/`gs`/`w` inside them recolours everything drawn afterwards. On arxiv p5, 10,210 of 10,342 pale-green table pixels turn black, and on USCIS N-400 p6 black rules are drawn white. Four of 17 wild PDFs change visibly (strict pass, below). No gate sees it, and the author cannot fix it from `translations.json`.
   - **Proposal:** inside BT…ET, drop only the text-state, positioning and showing operators. Keep `w J j M d ri i gs CS cs SC SCN sc scn G g RG rg K k`. Lock it with fill, stroke and Form-XObject fixtures. Because strip changes, rerun `dev/wild/probe.py` and a render diff. Ten corpus fixtures carry `w` inside BT, so their stripped bytes will change; their verdicts should not.
   - **Size:** S. **Existing ID:** none.

2. **R-02: the 4.0 pt clamp bypasses the scale floor.**
   - **Where:** `retypeset.py:1544, 1692, 1704, 1721`. The gate is fed the clamped ratio at `1546, 1694, 1706, 1723`; `consider_ratio` is at `1159-1171`.
   - **Why:** a 5 pt run that needs 0.54× is drawn at 4 pt and overlaps its neighbour by 15.8 pt. The report says 0.8 and verify exits 0; the threshold is 5.71 pt. A 3 pt source is drawn *larger* (30.4 pt overlap), and verify prints "everything ships at source size". This contradicts the module docstring (`:17-18`, `:39-40`) and the DECISIONS row of 2026-09-20.
   - **Proposal:** use `fs2 = fs * min(1, room / w)` with no lift, and pass the true ratio at all four sites. Add tests at 5 pt (must refuse without `allow_scale`) and at 3 pt (must never be enlarged).
   - **Size:** S. **Existing ID:** none.

3. **R-04: canonicalize every role face (REVIEW F4).**
   - **Where:** `retypeset.py:2034`.
   - **Why:** a legacy job that names separate italic or bold-italic files from Arial, Times New Roman, Georgia, Verdana or Courier New builds a correct page that verify then FAILs. The only workaround loses the italics the docs promise.
   - **Proposal:** use `list(dict.fromkeys([f_regular, f_bold, f_italic, f_bold_italic]))`. The verifier confirmed verify exits 0 with this for both Arial and Times. Lock it with the shared-glyph face that `tests/test_typography_retypeset.py:278-285` already builds.
   - **Size:** XS. **Existing ID:** REVIEW-2026-09-04 F4 (candidate row 34). It is parked behind P8, and P8's instrument was never built.

4. **R-47: stop reloading the page per glyph in typography verify.**
   - **Where:** `typography_verify.py:240` (inside the loop that starts at `:182`), combined with `field_fonts.py:129` (NeedAppearances).
   - **Why:** the documented step-7 verify of a delivered typography form peaks at 1,987 MB for the 3-page FL-100 and 4,101 MB for the 14-page N-400. Without NeedAppearances the same run peaks at 116 and 170 MB. Memory grows linearly with glyphs × widgets.
   - **Proposal:** add `page_rects = [p.rect for p in output]` next to `drawings` (`:124`) and index it. Measured with this change: 98 and 158 MB, with identical gate digests. Add a test that runs `run_field_fonts` and then verify.
   - **Size:** XS. **Existing ID:** none. typography-1 is opt-in.

5. **R-05: run the output-alias guard for legacy mappings.**
   - **Where:** `retypeset.py:1102-1111` (guard is typography-only), against `2001-2008` and `2019`; also `pipeline.py:494-498`.
   - **Why:** `scripts/retypeset.py stripped.pdf segments.json translations.json translations.json` exits 0 and has replaced the hand-authored mapping with PDF bytes. `rebuild` with `OUT=work/translations.json` destroys the file before it fails.
   - **Proposal:** resolve the fonts and run the protected-path check before the format branch. In `rebuild`, also check `out` against its inputs. Add tests for each alias.
   - **Size:** XS. **Existing ID:** none.

6. **R-06: escape non-tag text in inline-markup lines.**
   - **Where:** `retypeset.py:1607-1624` (the body is queued raw at `:1620`) and `1781-1784`.
   - **Why:** on any line that also carries `<b>`/`<i>`, `<usuario@ejemplo.com>` disappears, `&copy;` becomes ©, and `<span>` is swallowed. This contradicts `references/translations-format.md:39-42`.
   - **Proposal:** split on `INLINE_TAGS`, `html.escape` the pieces between tags, keep the `‖` removal, and add a locked test.
   - **Size:** XS. **Existing ID:** none.

7. **R-94: decide repository visibility now.**
   - **Where:** GitHub `ariasr47/pdf-translate-skill`; `docs/reviews/2026-09-19-public-readiness.md:34, 280`; `dev/goals/PROGRAM.md:77`.
   - **Why:** the repo is PUBLIC, and the event feed shows it has been since it was created on 2 September. The audit records it as private, and A20 frames sanitation as a step before publication. Public `main` carries the private product's milestone order and pin, a session log, the FL-150 job, and 31 files with workstation paths. No credentials or email addresses were found, and there is no sign of outside readers.
   - **Proposal:** Rodrigo decides now: either make the repo private until A20's review is done, or run A20 against the public tree and all 7 branches. Append a dated correction under the audit row and under A20. Any history rewrite still needs explicit approval.
   - **Size:** XS for the decision, M for the review. **Existing ID:** A20.

8. **R-03: key widget text by fully qualified field name.**
   - **Where:** `strip_text.py:424-425` (scaffold keeps the first widget per partial `/T`), `:594-600` (strip applies it to every match), `:472-475`, and `verify.py:1767-1785`.
   - **Why:** 72 widgets on 5 wild forms carry another field's accessible name; on FL-300 it is 57 of 212. Screen readers announce that name, and no gate compares `/TU` or `/V`. Following SKILL.md's identity rule for barcodes writes page 1's PDF417 `/V` onto every page of G-28, I-864 and N-400, which is 27 values.
   - **Proposal:** use the fully qualified name in the scaffold, in apply, and in `choice_exports`. Accept a partial name only when it is unique, and refuse when it is ambiguous. Land this together with F9/row 35 (the same keying in `--captions`) and F13/row 43. Add a two-parent fixture and a multi-page barcode fixture.
   - **Size:** M. It is here despite its size because it is high-impact and silent. **Existing ID:** none; F9 and F13 are its siblings.
