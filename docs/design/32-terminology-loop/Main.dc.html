The terminology loop
A wrong term of art cannot reach a delivery without a second reader, and every reviewed job makes the next one checkable.
Commands are named by subcommand below. Two spellings run: python3 scripts/pipeline.py …, which is what SKILL.md documents and what the thin wrappers exist for, and python3 -m pdf_translate.pipeline …. The consoles here use the package form.
qa --work DIR
unchanged
Mechanical defects in the mapping: figures and dates that moved, untranslated lines, inconsistent variants, bracket and punctuation parity. Advisory. It cannot read.
review --work DIR
new
Writes two files beside the mapping. Reads; changes nothing.
review_pairs.md
every authored pair, merges, overrides, notices
review_prompt.md
the MQM prompt, identity facts filled
A second reader fills review.json
A model first pass, a qualified human, or both. Outside the pipeline; the skill never calls a model. The schema is already references/review.md §4 — but §4 closes with “Nothing in the pipeline reads it”, and this row makes that line false. It has to change with the code.
review --work DIR --ingest review.json
new
Validates the schema, prints the open findings, and appends every accepted terminology finding to glossary.csv beside the job.
finish
now refuses
Refuses while review.json is absent, or any finding is open. --no-review is the only way past, and it prints one REVIEW line the delivery must carry.
Why — measured, 17 Sep 2026
The first FL-150 → Japanese job on skill v56 shipped with:
世帯主
for “head of household” — in Japan the registered household head, usually the married breadwinner; in US tax law a status reserved for the unmarried. On a form signed under penalty of perjury.
相手方の親
for “the other parent” — reads as the respondent’s own parent, the child’s grandparent.
verifyexited 0
qa_check0 errors
No gate could see either. The page was right and the strings were present.
What caught them
An independent MQM pass after delivery: 31 items — 1 critical, 6 major, 24 minor. 25 accepted, 6 rejected on measurement. A human page-1 read agreed with every accepted item.
The reviser was wrong twice in its top seven, so the loop measures the reviewer too.
The brief records 24 / 7; review.json reads 25 / 6, because one finding resolves as “accepted with a different fix”. The file is the measurement.
Not done when
A model is fine-tuned, or a global glossary ships inside the skill.
The reviser is presented as replacing human sign-off for court, government, medical or legal filings.
finish can pass an open finding without the REVIEW line in the delivery.
The terms-of-art table is optional prose rather than a slot the reviser reads.
