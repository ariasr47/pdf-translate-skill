references/terminology-failure-modes.md — new, named in SKILL.md step 1
The twin of failure-modes.md. Five categories, each with its FL-150 instance and its rule. Categories, not page facts — they transfer to every pair. Step 1 names both this file and the table above, so neither is optional prose.
1
Legal-status homonyms
A target word that names a different status in the target country. 世帯主 is the registered head of a 住民票 household — normally a married person; US head-of-household requires the filer to be unmarried.
Rule: a status the reader ticks about themselves is looked up in the target country’s own law before it is translated.
2
Party-role words reused inside compounds
相手方 was already the form’s word for RESPONDENT, so 相手方の親 grew a possessive reading — the respondent’s own parent. 他方の親 does not.
Rule: a term already bound to a party never appears inside a compound naming someone else.
3
Verbs of legal acts, chosen by object
“Executed” an order became 締結, the verb for concluding a contract.
Rule: the verb follows what is being acted on — an order, a deed, a will and a contract each take their own.
4
Head nouns narrower than their own examples
光熱費 excludes the water and trash its own parenthetical lists. 水道光熱費 covers them.
Rule: a head noun is checked against the examples in its own parenthetical.
5
Label sets mixing patterns
月額／週額／時給 — two of one pattern and one of another. A set shares one: 月給／週給／時給.
Rule: labels offered as a choice are authored together and read as a column, never one at a time.
