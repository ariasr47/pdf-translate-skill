## What separated them

**All five** produced a structurally sound file — every widget, `/Opt`
exports byte-identical, `verify` exit 0 as invoked, captions rewritten —
and every page I looked at kept its rules, leaders and columns. Rows 18,
19 and 23 hold: no caption off its rule, every list body on the source x.
Row 20 holds on the fixture: the quoted `/Title` inside three notices was
kept and named by the scan, and the school name became one phrase on the
command line; on the real form it did not fire (row 27).

**Sonnet lost the visual axis to a shrink the tool never mentioned.** Its
merged intro shipped at 0.81×; Opus's at 0.91×. Both deliveries say
nothing shrank, because retypeset reports a scale only below 0.7×. Opus
kept the axis on the strength of what it did find (zoomed crops, a 1 pt
leader gap, the dropdown in Chrome); Sonnet claimed inspection and named
nothing. On FL-100, Fable measured the band itself ("everything else at
82%–100%"). The tool's silence is a row (25).

**Haiku fell from 3 to 0.** No identity record, no lookup, the paper size
rewritten, no working-copy sentence, "all gates passed" beside an
unmentioned `qa_check` error. Every missing item lives in a part of the
skill P3 did not move, and its session was 87k tokens with no compaction:
this is the model, not the text. The user's judgement — Haiku is too weak
for this work — stands; it stays a canary for the skill text only.

**The real form is the headline.** Fable found the issuer's own Spanish
FL-100 S, let its terms win, put the notice where the issuer puts it in
the issuer's words, kept 158 fields, and disclosed every structural
decision including two 4 pt labels that a fillable layout forces where
the JC's static form does not. Beside FL-100 S it is the same document
with fields.

**Cost held.** Opus and Fable within a few percent of run 1; Sonnet in
the same band; Haiku a quarter of it; the real form twice the fixture.

## Defects, found by models rather than invented

1. **Ligatures in a merged paragraph corrupt the text layer** (Sonnet,
   Opus, and Fable on FL-100, independently; reproduced): U+FB01 or U+007F
   where "oficina" should be; a correct paragraph fails the placement gate
   under `--translations` and ships silently without it. **Row 24.**
2. **The unreported shrink band** (measured on Sonnet's and Opus's files):
   nothing between 0.7× and 1.0× is ever printed. **Row 25.**
3. **A merge that needs one more line** (Fable declined and hard-wrapped;
   Sonnet and Opus shrank; empty space right below): the author cannot
   give a merge a box. **Row 26.**
4. **The kept title must be tokenized like the run** (FL-100): `/Title`
   `FL-100 Petition—Marriage/Domestic Partnership` quoted verbatim in the
   notice is not kept because `FL` is under the word floor. **Row 27.**
5. **`right` shrinks before it anchors** (FL-100): a right-anchored label
   is scaled to the room right of the original origin, not to the room it
   will actually occupy. **Row 28.**
6. **An override-covered core still needs a plain value** (FL-100): the
   coverage check and the placement gate demand a translation that is
   never drawn. **Row 29.**
7. **The notice channel** (four of five runs wrote a script; Fable also in
   run 1). **P7** has its brief.

Recorded, not rowed: dot leaders start 1 pt after the label where the
source had 3 pt (Fable, Opus, at 300 dpi); `pipeline.py init` cannot
re-strip with `--widget-text` without re-extracting; a core shared
between roles has one translation and one alignment, so the author uses
`skip` plus overrides.

## What this says about the skill

The structural gates work on five runs, two fixture generations and one
real court form; the P3 cut cost nothing visible. What the gates cannot
do is still the story: notice a shrink they did not report, a ligature
they did not list, a paragraph that needed one more line, a title they
tokenized away. Each is now a measured row. Order: 24, 25, 27, 28, then
P7, 26, 29, then P4. The next lane A sitting is 24.
