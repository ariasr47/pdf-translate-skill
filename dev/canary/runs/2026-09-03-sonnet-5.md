## What it walked into

1. **A real defect: ligatures in a merged paragraph corrupt the text
   layer.** The Story engine applies the font's `fi` ligature, and the
   text layer then carries U+FB01 (with the full Noto Sans) or U+007F
   (with a subset that lost the ligature's code point — Opus saw that
   form). `canonicalize_text_layer` cannot reach it and the
   canonical-layer gate does not list it, so a correct paragraph with
   "oficina" or "firma" fails the placement gate loudly under
   `--translations` and ships silently without it. **Reproduced on a
   constructed merge:** five U+FB01 in the layer, "oficina" and "firma"
   absent verbatim, placement FAIL, canonical-layer PASS. Sonnet dodged it
   by rewording ("administración"); Opus stripped the `liga` features
   from the font before subsetting. Two models, independently, on the
   same evening. **Row 24.**
