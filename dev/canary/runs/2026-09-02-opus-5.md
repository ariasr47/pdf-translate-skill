## Defect 1 — the placement gate cannot pass a wrapped merge (fixed)

Its report: *"`verify --translations` compares a merge's `html` verbatim
against the output text layer, but a re-flowed merge wraps, so `get_text()`
returns a newline at the wrap point. Any Latin merge longer than one line
fails an otherwise-correct build."*

Reproduced on a constructed two-line paragraph:

```
retypeset rc = 0        no overflow, placed at full size
layer:  '...declaraciones presentadas\nantes de la fecha...'
target: '...declaraciones presentadas antes de la fecha...'
verify rc = 1           FAIL missing translation targets (1)
```

This broke paragraph mode — the whole point of the merge machinery, and of
the "manuals and brochures" claim. **Fixed the same sitting**, because a
gate that fails correct output is the bug by the program's own loop:
`normalize_ws` collapses wrapping whitespace (newline, tab, runs of ASCII
spaces) on both sides and folds nothing else. NBSP and the hyphen variants
stay unfolded — drift belongs to the canonical-text-layer gate, and folding
it here is what used to hide it. Locked by `MergePlacementGateTests`,
including a merge that really is missing and must still fail.

Opus worked around it by re-authoring the paragraph as two segment
translations split where the source line-breaks. That is a worse document —
it hard-codes the source's wrap points into the target — and it should not
have had to.

## Defect 2 — list markers lose the source's gap (recorded, not fixed)

Its report: retypeset re-emits `marker + ' '` where the source had two
spaces, shifting every list body left. Measured on this fixture:

| marker | source gap | retypeset writes | shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1.` `2.` `3.` `4.` `5.` | 15.29 pt | 12.23 pt | **3.06 pt left** |

Exactly the figure it reported. Systematic: it hits every numbered or
lettered list whose source aligns bodies with more than one space, and the
bodies no longer line up with anything else on the page. Opus corrected it
with five `overrides` at the measured original columns — the right move
available to it, and not one an author should have to make.

**Its own sitting**, not today's: the fix means recording the marker's
trailing whitespace in the segment and re-emitting it, which touches the
schema and every placement path.
